New: Interaction Types

🧩 Fill-in-the-Blank
Melody plays with gaps. You fill in the missing notes. PianoRoll shows greyed-out placeholder bars where notes are missing.
Skills: melodic prediction, pattern recognition
🔍 Spot the Error
Hear a melody with one wrong note. Tap the key where the error was. Multiple choice: 4 highlighted keys, pick the wrong one.
Skills: critical listening, pitch accuracy
🎲 Interval Quiz
Hear two notes. Identify the interval (minor 3rd, perfect 5th, etc.) by tapping a button. Or: given an interval name, play it from a root note.
Skills: interval recognition, theory
🏗️ Chord Builder
Given a root note + chord type (e.g. "Build Cm7"), construct the chord note by note. Visual feedback shows which notes are correct as you add them.
Skills: chord theory, voicings
🎼 Key Signature ID
Hear a short passage. Identify the key (multiple choice: 4 options). Or: see a key signature on staff, play the scale.
Skills: key recognition, scales

New: Gamified Wrappers

⚔️ Boss Battle
Tier-end epic challenge. Boss cat throws modifiers: tempo ramps, keys go dark, notes flip. 3 lives. Defeat = rare loot chest.
Appears: End of each tier (15 total)
🎹 Duet
Your cat plays left hand, you play right (or vice versa). Cat's part complexity matches their evolution stage. Cooperative scoring.
Appears: Bonus exercises in lessons
🏃 Speed Run
Timed exercise chain. 5 short exercises, clock ticking. Stars based on time + accuracy. Leaderboard per tier.
Appears: Daily challenges
🎯 Endless Mode
How far can you go without a mistake? Progressive difficulty. Notes get faster, patterns get harder. Personal best tracking.
Appears: Unlocked per tier as reward
🎤 Teacher Challenge
Salsa says "Can you play this faster/louder/smoother?" Specific technique goals with cat personality commentary. Friendly competition.
Appears: Daily sessions

New: Creative Types

🎸 Improvisation
Given a chord progression + scale, play freely for 8-16 bars. Scoring: % notes in-key, rhythmic variety, use of chord tones. AI feedback on musicality. Keys outside the scale are dimmed.
Appears: Unlocked at tier 10+ | Skills: creativity, scale application, musical expression
